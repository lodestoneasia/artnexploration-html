The process

From a feeling to a painting

  1. 01

    Conversation

    Tell Parames about the space, the feeling you want to live with, colours you love, and any story or meaning behind the piece.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    You receive a proposal with size, palette direction, timeline and price. Once you are happy, your slot is confirmed.

  3. 03

    Creation

    Parames paints your piece. Abstract work is intuitive, so there is room for the painting to surprise you both.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Your finished, signed original is delivered and ready to hang.

Timeline & pricing

Every commission is different

Pricing and timeline depend on the size and complexity of the work. As a guide, Parames’s available originals range from RM500 for a 30 cm canvas to RM8,000 for a large multi-panel piece.
