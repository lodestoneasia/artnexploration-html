About this work

The Sidrat al-Muntaha, the Lote-Tree beyond which there is no passing, is an ancient symbol of the boundary between the human and the Divine. Here it blazes with golden light among swirling storm clouds.

The artist

“I’ve been painting for more than a decade. Being an artist is a tug-of-war between my inner dreamer and my inner critic, and I have grown to love that process. It has taught me about the world, and about myself.”
