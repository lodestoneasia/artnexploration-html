Breathe in

Why abstract art

We are all one in essence, and every child can make art. Abstract painting removes the “correct answer”, so children who find handwriting, focus or self-expression hard can succeed on their own terms.

01

Fine-motor strength

Big brushes, palette knives, pouring and scraping build hand control without the pressure of handwriting. Particularly helpful for children with dysgraphia.

02

Calm & regulation

Slow, sensory mark-making helps children notice and settle their feelings.

03

Planning & focus

Building a painting in layers trains sequencing, patience and executive function.

04

Confidence

No mistakes, only choices. Children finish each term with a series of finished work and a small exhibition.
