My canvas · Spirituality, service & exploration

Abstract forms and varied textures become a visual narrative, inviting you to explore the universal themes held in each piece.

I’ve been painting for more than a decade. Being an artist is a tug-of-war between my inner dreamer and my inner critic, and I have grown to love that process. It has taught me about the world, and about myself.

Parames works in acrylic on mounted canvas, pouring, scraping, layering and drawing into wet paint. Her work moves between pure abstraction and quiet, devotional images drawn from many faiths.
